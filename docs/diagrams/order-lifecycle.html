Process · ProcessNow

From material in to money collected

The vendor never logs in — company staff record every step. Price and cost are frozen onto the order item the moment it is created, which is why a price change tomorrow cannot rewrite a bill from last month.

The decision

Bills are snapshots

  • Creating an order item copies unitPrice, unitCost, the chosen options and billOn off the service type
  • Editing a service type later never rewrites an old bill — the numbers already live on the item
  • Prices come from the database, never from the request; the frontend's preview is display only

At the return

One transaction

  • Quantity out, line amounts, the returned status and the bill are all written in a single transaction
  • billOn decides whether the line bills on quantity in or quantity out, per service type
  • Payments may be partial; amount due is derived (total − paid), never stored as a status

Not drawn

Cut for density

  • Cancel — allowed only from received or processing; once a bill exists the money is corrected through the bill
  • Void — only a bill with no payments can be voided; it then owes nothing
  • Per-company orderNo / billNo from atomic counters, optionally prefixed (FN-0001)
  • Money is Decimal in the database and 2-decimal strings on the wire; quantities keep their own precision
